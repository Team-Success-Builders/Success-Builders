Every great experiment needs the right variables.

This BIOSA election is no different. Team Success Builders is a team that doesn’t just promise change, it comes with a formula for RESULTS.

We are built on teamwork, unity, leadership, student empowerment and collective progress. We believe meaningful progress comes through collaboration, effective representation and a shared commitment to success.

The Results Agenda

R.E.S.U.L.T.S. — our formula for progress

Seven pillars. One clear direction. This is what we will work on for every student in the Department of Biology.

R

Representation

A strong, visible voice for students in every room where decisions are made.

E

Educational Excellence

Better academic support, resources and opportunities for stronger results.

S

Student Welfare

No one left behind. Your needs matter and your wellbeing comes first.

U

Unity & Development

One BIOSA, one family. We grow stronger when we grow together.

L

Leadership with Accountability & Integrity

Leaders who answer to students and do what they say.

T

Transparency & Innovation

Open processes, clear decisions and fresh ideas for better systems.

S

Scientific & Career Development

Connecting you to skills, labs, mentors and pathways that launch your career in science.
